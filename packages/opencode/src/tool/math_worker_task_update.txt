Replace an existing detached math-worker's TASK with a new self-contained assignment. For a running worker, the in-flight round is fenced as superseded and the next round reads the new TASK. For a content-blocked lane, revise the TASK substantively and then call math_worker_ensure on the same durable session ID. This does not clear a deliberate stop marker. The TASK must be non-empty, at most 100000 characters, and free of @file pointer references.
