# PURPOSE
Scroll the page currently open in the embedded desktop browser.

Use this to reach content outside the viewport before a `browser_read` or
`browser_screenshot` — the visible viewport only, no full-page capture needed.

# GUIDELINES
- Without arguments the page wheels down by roughly one viewport height.
- `direction: "up"` wheels back toward the top of the page.
- `amount` is the wheel distance in pixels (positive number).
- Pass a `uid` from the most recent `browser_read` snapshot to scroll a
  specific element into view (e.g. before clicking something off-screen);
  `direction` and `amount` are ignored when `uid` is given.
- Scrolling can trigger lazy loading or infinite lists: use `browser_read` to read the newly revealed content.
